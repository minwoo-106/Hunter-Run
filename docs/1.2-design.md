# 1.2 — indoor activity and operator conversation

## Indoor rules

Activity environment is separate from exploration/contract/gate mode. Optional Codable fields preserve old saves as outdoor sessions. Stationary exercise and treadmill modes use the same active-time progression, quests, rank eligibility and boss campaign settlement. They never invent GPS distance and never modify outdoor pace learning. Distance goals are not offered indoors. A 30-second motion-maintenance alternative replaces pursuit, endurance and boss acceleration instructions while retaining encounter identity and reward settlement.

SensorProbe does not start Core Location indoors. CMPedometer provides actual counted steps; a pure IndoorMovementTracker accepts fresh step evidence or a regular sequence of user-acceleration pulses for phone-in-hand exercise. Single shakes, stationary noise, delivery gaps and expired evidence do not qualify. This is motion evidence, not proof of physiological exercise or measured running speed. Its thresholds require physical-phone feedback for different grips and gait patterns. Sensor traces are processed locally, not saved or uploaded.

A foreground preflight checks real movement before entry. Watch and HealthKit are not required. Permission denial and unavailable hardware block entry rather than awarding timer-only progress. Background/lock/interruption and manual pause stop indoor sensors; resume starts fresh evidence. Recovery keeps the environment, counters and in-flight event while requiring explicit resume. Pedometer updates may lag and rhythm-only activity can advance time without increasing the displayed step count.

Combat instructions reuse the approved neutral resume recording through the logical indoor_hold cue, alongside the explicit on-screen countdown. Existing 113 WAVs remain unchanged. All outdoor behavior keeps the existing sensor and acceleration paths.

## Conversation presentation

The operator occupies the scene with a single bottom dialogue panel. A tap advances a line to two or three contextual player replies. Choices disappear after answering; short follow-up exchanges replace the topic dashboard. Name and current line remain in the dialogue panel. Affinity and relationship chapters move to a separate book control. Approved portraits and six expressions are reused; drink and relationship preferences retain the existing persisted keys and unlock constraints. Repeat casual conversations grant no farmable affinity.

The dialogue/choice separation references [Ren'Py dialogue](https://www.renpy.org/doc/html/dialogue.html) and [in-game choice menus](https://www.renpy.org/doc/html/menus.html); text and artwork are original. Sensor access follows [Apple CMPedometer](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmpedometer).

The title subtitle now uses light parchment text with a dark shadow instead of low-contrast gold.
